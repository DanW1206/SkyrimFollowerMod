# SkyrimFollowerMod

A follower mod developed for Skyrim Special Edition.
| Does not requite SKSE / is base game compatible | Uses custom textures, meshes, and animations |

Creating this mod was a journey. Before starting, I had no experience with Bethesda's Creator Kit, the Papyrus Scripting language, or working knowledge of animating
and texture design + rendering. And admittedly I may have bit off more than I could chew in the beginning. However, these same dreams and standards are what pushed me to 
dive into all of the above and more. While obviously still learning, my confidence has grown exponentially in these technologies and I am now looking forward to test the
waters of more complex mod ideas in the future. 

For rendering and animating textures, I utilized GIMP 3.08-0
https://www.gimp.org/news/2026/01/24/gimp-3-0-8-released/

For creating animation logic and exporting files as .dds for Creator Kit, I used NifSkope 1.4.2
https://www.nifskope.com/about-us/

And for everything else, I used the SSE's Creator Kit (with SSEedit to verify file structures).
https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/164
